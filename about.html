About root_n_reels

root_n_reels is a practical technical reference library for system administrators, infrastructure engineers, DevOps learners, and IT professionals preparing for real-world operations or technical interviews.

What you will find here

  • Linux and RHEL administration references.
  • UNIX resources covering Solaris and HP-UX.
  • Networking, Nutanix, server hardware, Podman and Ansible resources.
  • Scenario-based interview preparation and troubleshooting guidance.
  • Structured learning paths designed for self-paced study.

Our approach

Content is organized around practical concepts, useful commands, operational workflows, and interview-focused explanations. The goal is to make complex infrastructure topics easier to explore and revisit.

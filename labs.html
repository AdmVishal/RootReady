Practice Labs

Try the scenario first, then reveal the suggested approach.

1. Filesystem is 95% full

Which commands would you run first?

df -h, then du -xhd1 /path | sort -h; check logs, deleted-open files, and cleanup policy.

2. Service is not responding

How do you investigate?

Use systemctl status, journalctl -u SERVICE, process checks, listening ports, dependencies, and recent changes.

3. Load average is high

What evidence do you collect?

Collect uptime, top, vmstat 1 5, iostat -xz 1 5, memory, I/O wait, and process-level data.
